Hi! I'm Eva, and KAVE is the blog of my creative journey :)

Ever since I was a child, I've been drawn to every way I could express myself — watercolor, pencil, gouache — before finding my way to graphic design, sewing, knitting, and embroidery.

Everything shown here is made completely from scratch, patterns and templates included, because a piece only feels like mine when I've built it without an outside source.

Hopefully my creative journey inspires you to continue — or start — your own.

“The meaning of life is your work. The material the earth offers you, and what you make of it.”
